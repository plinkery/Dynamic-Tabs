# Privacy Policy for Dynamic Tabs

Dynamic Tabs respects your privacy. 

- **Data Storage:** All collection data, tab records, and timestamps are stored strictly locally on your device using `chrome.storage.local`.
- **Data Transmission:** Dynamic Tabs does not collect, track, transmit, or share any personal browsing history, tab information, or analytics with external servers.
- **Permissions:** Broad host access (`<all_urls>`) is strictly used locally within your browser to detect Single Page Application (SPA) navigation changes and update tracked saved tabs.
